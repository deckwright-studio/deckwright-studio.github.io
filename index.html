How it works

  1. You send what you have: text, numbers, a logo, photos. A document or a few bullet points in a message is enough.
  2. We build the deck and send you a preview of every slide.
  3. You tell us what to change. Two rounds of changes are included.
  4. When you approve the preview, you get the final files.

Everything about prices, payment and timing is in our terms of service.
